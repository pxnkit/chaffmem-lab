# Code of conduct

## Our standard

ChaffMem Lab welcomes careful disagreement and reproducible criticism. Contributors are expected to discuss methods, evidence, and limitations without attacking people.

Positive behavior includes:

- Giving specific and actionable review feedback
- Declaring uncertainty and conflicts of interest
- Crediting prior work and data sources
- Respecting privacy and safety boundaries
- Reporting negative results honestly

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information
- Misrepresenting another person's work or results
- Pressuring contributors to hide strong baselines or failed hypotheses
- Using project channels to coordinate testing against systems without authorization

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner.
